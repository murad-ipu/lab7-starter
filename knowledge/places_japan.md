# Places in Japan (日本の観光地)

## Chuson-ji (中尊寺) — Hiraizumi, Iwate (岩手県平泉町)
- Category: temple / 寺, UNESCO World Heritage (世界遺産), Tohoku (東北)
- Highlights: Konjikido golden hall (金色堂) built under the Northern Fujiwara clan (奥州藤原氏); quiet cedar-lined approach path (参道)
- Access: JR Tohoku Main Line to Hiraizumi Station (平泉駅), about 9 min from Ichinoseki, then Iwate-ken Kotsu bus or the town loop bus "Runrun" (るんるん)
- Cost: admission 1,000 yen for adults, covers Konjikido and the treasure hall (拝観料 1,000円)
- Best season: autumn leaves / 紅葉 (the approach is lit up in autumn); open 8:30–17:00 (until 16:30 from Nov 4 to end of Feb)
- Source: https://www.chusonji.or.jp/ (checked 2026-11)

## Yamadera (山寺) — Yamagata City, Yamagata (山形県山形市)
- Category: temple / 寺 (Risshaku-ji 立石寺), mountain hike / 山登り, Tohoku (東北)
- Highlights: climb 1,015 stone steps (石段) to the Godaido viewpoint (五大堂) over the valley; Konponchudo main hall (根本中堂) is an Important Cultural Property
- Access: JR Senzan Line to Yamadera Station (山寺駅), then about 10 min on foot to the stair entrance
- Cost: entry 500 yen for junior high school age and up, cash only (入山料 500円)
- Best season: autumn leaves / 紅葉 and fresh green in early summer; round trip takes about 1.5 hours, last entry earlier in winter
- Source: https://www.rissyakuji.jp/ (checked 2026-11)

## Ginzan Onsen (銀山温泉) — Obanazawa, Yamagata (山形県尾花沢市)
- Category: hot spring / 温泉 town, ryokan (旅館), Tohoku (東北)
- Highlights: wooden Taisho-era (大正ロマン) inns lining both sides of a narrow river; gas lamps make the street glow at night
- Access: JR Ou Line to Oishida Station (大石田駅), bus to Obanazawa, change to the bus for Ginzan Onsen (about 40 min); by car about 30 min from Obanazawa IC
- Cost: no entry fee to walk the street; day-bathing and inn prices vary by ryokan (estimate)
- Best season: winter snow / 雪景色 (very crowded, plan ahead)
- Source: https://www.ginzanonsen.jp/access/ (checked 2026-11)

## Zuigan-ji (瑞巌寺) — Matsushima, Miyagi (宮城県松島町)
- Category: temple / 寺, National Treasure (国宝), Zen (禅), Tohoku (東北)
- Highlights: Date Masamune's family temple facing Matsushima Bay (松島湾) with its pine-covered islands; treasure museum (宝物館)
- Access: JR Senseki Line to Matsushima-Kaigan Station (松島海岸駅), about 10 min on foot; no temple parking
- Cost: admission 1,000 yen for adults (拝観料 1,000円)
- Best season: autumn / 秋; opens 8:30, closing time changes by month (15:30 in Dec–Jan, 17:00 in Apr–Sep)
- Source: https://www.zuiganji.or.jp/guide/ (checked 2026-11)

## Nikko Toshogu (日光東照宮) — Nikko, Tochigi (栃木県日光市)
- Category: shrine / 神社, UNESCO World Heritage (世界遺産)
- Highlights: richly carved Yomeimon gate (陽明門); the sleeping cat carving (眠猫) and the "singing dragon" ceiling (鳴龍)
- Access: Tobu limited express from Asakusa to Tobu-Nikko Station (東武日光駅), about 1 h 50 min, then bus or walk (time from the station not checked)
- Cost: admission 1,600 yen for adults; 2,400 yen with the treasure house (宝物館)
- Best season: autumn leaves / 紅葉; open 9:00–17:00 (Apr–Oct) and 9:00–16:00 (Nov–Mar)
- Source: https://www.toshogu.jp/pages/25/ (checked 2026-11)

## Senso-ji (浅草寺) — Asakusa, Taito, Tokyo (東京都台東区)
- Category: temple / 寺, old town (下町)
- Highlights: the big red lantern at Kaminarimon gate (雷門), Nakamise shopping street (仲見世) for snacks and souvenirs, five-storey pagoda (五重塔)
- Access: Asakusa Station (浅草駅) on the Tokyo Metro Ginza Line, Toei Asakusa Line, Tobu or Tsukuba Express, about 5 min on foot
- Cost: free to enter the grounds (not stated on the official site; estimate)
- Best season: all year; New Year visit (初詣) is very crowded; main hall open 6:00–17:00 (from 6:30 Oct–Mar)
- Source: https://www.senso-ji.jp/access/ (checked 2026-11)

## Shirakawa-go (白川郷) — Shirakawa, Gifu (岐阜県白川村)
- Category: historic village / 集落, UNESCO World Heritage (世界遺産), farmhouses (合掌造り)
- Highlights: more than 100 steep thatched-roof gassho-zukuri houses (合掌造り) in Ogimachi (荻町); view from the hill over the village
- Access: no train station; expressway bus from Takayama (about 50 min) or Kanazawa (about 1 h 20 min) to Shirakawa-go bus terminal
- Cost: no village entry fee listed; car parking 2,000 yen; some houses charge to go inside (estimate)
- Best season: winter snow / 雪 (winter tyres needed if driving)
- Source: https://shirakawa-go.gr.jp/access/ (checked 2026-11)

## Kenroku-en (兼六園) — Kanazawa, Ishikawa (石川県金沢市)
- Category: garden / 庭園, one of Japan's three great gardens (日本三名園)
- Highlights: the two-legged Kotoji stone lantern (徽軫灯籠) by the pond; snow ropes (雪吊り) on the pine trees in winter
- Access: bus from JR Kanazawa Station (金沢駅) (route and time not checked on the official site)
- Cost: admission 320 yen for adults 18 and over (入園料 320円)
- Best season: cherry blossom / 桜 and autumn leaves / 紅葉, with night illumination; open 7:00–18:00 (Mar–mid Oct), 8:00–17:00 (mid Oct–Feb)
- Source: https://shiro-niwa.pref.ishikawa.lg.jp/kenrokuen-garden/info.html (checked 2026-11)

## Fushimi Inari Taisha (伏見稲荷大社) — Fushimi, Kyoto (京都府京都市伏見区)
- Category: shrine / 神社, Inari fox shrine (お稲荷さん)
- Highlights: tunnels of red torii gates (鳥居) climbing Mount Inari (稲荷山); fox statues (狐) at the gates
- Access: right next to JR Nara Line Inari Station (稲荷駅), about 5 min from Kyoto Station; or 5 min walk from Keihan Fushimi-Inari Station
- Cost: free (no fee listed on the official site; estimate)
- Best season: early morning all year to avoid crowds
- Source: https://inari.jp/access/ (checked 2026-11)

## Kinkaku-ji (金閣寺) — Kita, Kyoto (京都府京都市北区)
- Category: temple / 寺 (Rokuon-ji 鹿苑寺), Zen (禅), UNESCO World Heritage (世界遺産)
- Highlights: the Golden Pavilion (金閣) with gold leaf on its top two floors, reflected in Kyoko-chi pond (鏡湖池)
- Access: Kyoto City Bus 12, 59 or 205 to Kinkakuji-michi (金閣寺道)
- Cost: admission 500 yen for high school age and up (拝観料 500円)
- Best season: winter snow on the gold pavilion (雪の金閣) and autumn leaves / 紅葉; open 9:00–17:00 every day
- Source: https://www.shokoku-ji.jp/kinkakuji/access/ (checked 2026-11)

## Todai-ji (東大寺) — Nara City, Nara (奈良県奈良市)
- Category: temple / 寺, UNESCO World Heritage (世界遺産), Great Buddha (大仏)
- Highlights: the huge bronze Great Buddha (大仏) inside the Daibutsuden hall (大仏殿); wild deer (鹿) in Nara Park around the temple
- Access: about 20 min on foot from Kintetsu Nara Station (近鉄奈良駅), or loop bus to Todaiji Daibutsuden stop then 5 min walk
- Cost: Great Buddha Hall 800 yen for adults, cash only; 1,200 yen with the Todai-ji Museum
- Best season: autumn / 秋; hall open 7:30–17:30 (Apr–Oct) and 8:00–17:00 (Nov–Mar)
- Source: https://www.todaiji.or.jp/information/haikan/ (checked 2026-11)

## Himeji Castle (姫路城) — Himeji, Hyogo (兵庫県姫路市)
- Category: castle / 城, National Treasure (国宝), UNESCO World Heritage (世界遺産)
- Highlights: original white plaster keep (白鷺城, "White Heron Castle"), lit up at night; steep wooden stairs inside the keep
- Access: about 20 min on foot from JR Himeji Station (姫路駅), or Shinki bus to Otemon-mae then 5 min walk
- Cost: 2,500 yen for visitors 18 and over (1,000 yen for Himeji residents); 2,600 yen with Kokoen garden
- Best season: cherry blossom / 桜 in spring; open 9:00–17:00 (last entry 16:00)
- Source: https://www.himejicastle.jp/ (checked 2026-11)

## Itsukushima Shrine (厳島神社) — Miyajima, Hatsukaichi, Hiroshima (広島県廿日市市)
- Category: shrine / 神社, UNESCO World Heritage (世界遺産), island (島)
- Highlights: the great torii gate (大鳥居) standing in the sea; shrine halls seem to float at high tide (満潮); arched bridge (反橋)
- Access: JR or Hiroden to Miyajimaguchi (宮島口), then about 10 min by ferry (フェリー) to Miyajima pier
- Cost: shrine entry 300 yen for adults (昇殿料 300円); 500 yen with the treasure hall
- Best season: autumn leaves / 紅葉 at Momijidani; open from 6:30, closing 17:00–18:00 depending on season
- Source: https://www.itsukushimajinja.jp/jp/admission.html (checked 2026-11)
